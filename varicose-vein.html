Varicose Vein

What is a varicose vein?

The supplied chart is titled “Varicose Vein” and illustrates varicose veins, perforator incompetence, skin changes, venous ulceration, reticular veins and thread veins. It does not provide a written definition, so no additional clinical definition has been attributed to the source chart.
