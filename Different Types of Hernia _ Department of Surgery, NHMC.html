Different Types of Hernia

What is a hernia?

A hernia is an abnormal protrusion of a viscus, or a part of a viscus, through an opening, natural or artificial, with a sac covering it.

In simple words: an organ or part of an organ pushes out through an opening, and the sac covers it.

Types explained

1. Groin hernias

Hernias at the groin.

Indirect inguinal hernia

The commonest type of hernia. It starts at the opening of the inguinal canal.

Direct inguinal hernia

Appears near the opening of the inguinal canal.

Femoral hernia

Occurs in the femoral canal, below the groin.

2. Abdominal wall hernias

Hernias of the front wall of the abdomen.

Umbilical hernia

At the navel. It can occur in a child.

Incisional hernia

At the site of a previous surgical incision. It can become very large.

3. Large hernias

Hernias that grow big.

Giant hernia

A very large hernia.

Large direct hernia with umbilical hernia

More than one hernia can be present in the same patient.
