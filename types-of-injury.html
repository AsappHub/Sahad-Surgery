Types of Injury

What is an injury?

An injury is damage to the body. The supplied chart organizes injuries by their causes, severity, and the affected part of the body.

The chart classifies injuries in 3 ways

  1. Based on Causes
  2. Based on Severity
  3. Based on Affected Part of Body

Classification explained

1. Based on Causes

This section groups injuries by how they happen.

Traumatic Injuries

Fractures, dislocation, sprains and strains, concussions.

Overuse Injuries

Tendinitis, stress fractures, carpal tunnel syndrome.

Burn Injuries

Chemical, thermal, electricity.

Penetrating Injuries

Stab wounds, gunshot wounds, puncture wounds.

2. Based on Severity

This section groups injuries by the severity described in the chart.

Mild Injuries

Bruises, minor cuts, muscles strains.

Moderate Injuries

Deep cuts, ligament tears, minor fractures.

Severe Injuries

Major fractures, head trauma, internal bleeding.

3. Based on Affected Part of Body

This section groups injuries by the body part affected.

Head Injuries

Concussion, skull fractures, traumatic brain injuries.

Spinal Injuries

Herniated discs, spinal cord damage, paralysis.

Musculoskeletal Injuries

Fractures, dislocations, sprains and strains.

Internal Injuries

Organ damage, internal bleeding.
